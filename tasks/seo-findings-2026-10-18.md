# etownkyrealtors.com — search visibility findings (2026-10-18)

Source: DataForSEO Labs + live SERP pulls, 2026-10-18. Raw pulls in session scratchpad.

## Headline: the site ranks as a weak tourism site, not a real estate directory

**65 ranked keywords total. Zero in positions 1-3. Three in 4-10.**

| Bucket | Count |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | 0 |
| 4-10 | 3 |
| 11-20 | 5 |
| 21-50 | 45 |
| 51+ | 12 |

Of 111 pages, roughly 8 distinct URLs rank for anything. The 14 neighborhood, 9 school,
7 park and 9 realtor pages are effectively invisible.

`/elizabethtown/things-to-do/` alone accounts for ~30 of the 65 keywords. The competitor
set DataForSEO returns is therefore dominated by tourism sites — tripadvisor, touretown.com,
downtownetown.org, kentuckytourism.com, thecrazytourist, letsroam, wanderlog. The only
genuine real-estate competitor with meaningful overlap is **olive-oak.com** (Olive + Oak
Realty — already profiled on this site).

## The site's real strength is Fort Knox / relocation content

Best positions on the whole site: "bah fort knox 2026" **p9**, "fort knox zip code bah" **p9**,
"elizabethtown ky cost of living" **p9**, "cost of living in elizabethtown ky" **p11**,
"is elizabethtown ky a good place to live" **p12**, "bah fort knox" / "fort knox bah" **p13**.

These are informational intents that need no listings feed. This is the winnable territory —
and it is also the most time-decaying content on the site (2027 BAH rates publish ~Dec 2026).

## Trap: "homes for sale elizabethtown ky" (5,400/mo, KD 2) is NOT winnable with static content

KD 2 makes this look like the easiest win on the board. The live SERP says otherwise — the
top 15 is **100% live-inventory portals**, every one showing a listing count:

realtor.com (#1, "365 single family homes") · exprealty (#2) · century21 (#3) ·
zillow (#4, #5, #8) · buyowner (#6) · facebook (#7) · trulia (#9) · hardinhomes.com (#11,
local IDX, "All homes from all local Realtors") · forsalebyowner (#13) · redfin (#14)

Not one static content page ranks. The barrier is not link difficulty, it is **content type** —
Google serves a listings SERP for this query. A static export with no IDX/MLS feed cannot
satisfy it. Same applies to the "houses for rent elizabethtown ky" (1,900/mo, KD 3) and
"elizabethtown ky apartments" (2,900/mo) clusters.

**Do not build static pages against these.** Winning them requires an IDX/MLS integration,
which is a different project with a different cost structure.

## The real opportunity: own the directory/comparison intent

The SERP for "realtors in elizabethtown ky" is winnable by content, and competitors are
already doing it:

| # | Result | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | homelight.com — "Top Real Estate Agents in Elizabethtown, KY **(August 2026)**" | Comparison page with a **dated title** |
| 7 | olive-oak.com — "/blog/best-real-estate-agents-in-elizabethtown-ky" | A competitor brokerage outranking us with one blog post |
| 2, 6, 12, 13 | c21bluegrassrealtypros, pennprops, janesrealtygroup, rainbowrealtyky | Small local brokerage homepages |
| 3, 9, 14, 15 | hkar.com ×4 | The local Realtor association directory |

This site **is** a realtor directory with 9 brokerage profiles and it sits at p28-42, beaten
by a single competitor blog post. The format that wins is a dated, substantive comparison page
backed by credible individual profiles.

## Priorities this implies

1. **Fix the thin brokerage profiles** (112-139 words vs the client's own 1,266). The directory
   cannot win a directory SERP while its entries are the weakest pages on the site.
2. **Build a "Best Realtors in Elizabethtown KY" comparison page** — the format demonstrably
   ranking at #5 and #7.
3. **Defend and refresh the Fort Knox BAH cluster** — the site's only near-page-one asset, and
   the one with a hard expiry when 2027 rates publish in December.
4. **Add visible freshness** — the #5 result literally dates its title.
5. **Do not** chase listings intents with static pages.

## Update 2026-10-18 (after verification)

The "fix the thin brokerage profiles" priority above turned out to understate the
problem. Checking all eight against the Heart of Kentucky Association of REALTORS
office roster (160 member offices) found four were materially wrong and two of
those described businesses that could not be confirmed to exist at all.

The directory now carries **five verified competitors** plus the client, rather
than eight entries of which half were inaccurate:

| Kept (verified against HKAR) | Removed |
|---|---|
| Schuler Bauer ERA Powered | Greystone Realtors — no roster, registry or web presence |
| Olive + Oak Realty | Network Realty — not in roster, details unconfirmable |
| RE/MAX Executive Group | Century 21 Advantage — real, but located in London KY |
| Keller Williams Heartland | |
| Coldwell Banker McMahan *(was listed as "Legacy Group")* | |

All removals 301 to `/realtors/`; the Coldwell Banker slug 301s to its new path.

**This strengthens rather than weakens the Phase 3 case.** The competing pages
that rank for the directory intent — homelight.com at #5, olive-oak.com at #7 —
win on credibility and freshness, not on entry count. A directory of five
verifiable brokerages with confirmed addresses is a better foundation for a
"Best Realtors in Elizabethtown KY" comparison page than eight entries where
half the contact details were wrong.

Worth noting for Phase 3: **Olive + Oak is both a directory entry here and the
strongest organic competitor**, ranking above this site for its core commercial
term with a single blog post. The comparison page has to treat that honestly.
